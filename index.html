How to read these numbers

The median is the midpoint when qualifying sale prices are ordered from lowest to highest. Sales under $10,000 are excluded because the dataset notes that some very low prices are implausible. The rule is applied consistently to every city. Prices are shown in whole dollars.

About the data

Completed home sales from 18 college towns. Values are as reported by the collection source, not independently verified against county records.
